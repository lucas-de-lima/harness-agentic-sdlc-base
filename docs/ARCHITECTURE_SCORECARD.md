# Architecture Scorecard

## Purpose

Provide structured comparison without pretending architecture is a mathematical optimization problem.

## Dimensions

Score each candidate from 1 to 5:

- domain fit
- complexity fit
- integration fit
- persistence fit
- concurrency fit
- testability
- operational burden
- evolvability

## Weighting

Weights are advisory:

- domain fit: 20%
- complexity fit: 20%
- operational burden: 15%
- testability: 15%
- integration fit: 10%
- persistence fit: 10%
- concurrency fit: 5%
- evolvability: 5%

## Override rule

A high aggregate score does not override a major architectural mismatch.

The final decision must explain any significant score/discretion mismatch.
